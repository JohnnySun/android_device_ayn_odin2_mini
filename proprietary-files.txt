# TouchMapping is deliberately absent. Its native library talks to /dev/rscom
# and /dev/rsinput, which this port does not have: the kernel registers only
# rstouch and rsgpio, and the controller UART belongs to rsinputd. The app
# therefore fails device_cmd_ctl forever, burning about 30 percent of a core
# and writing over two thousand log lines a second. It is a persistent system
# app, so disabling and killing it on the device does not stop it. Touch
# mapping is an unported feature, not a working one.
